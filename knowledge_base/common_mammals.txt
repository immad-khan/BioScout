The Margalla Hills support a range of mammalian life, from small rodents to larger predators. The most notable large mammal is the Common Leopard (Panthera pardus), though sightings are rare. Other commonly observed mammals include the Indian Pangolin, Wild Boar, Barking Deer (Muntjac), and various species of civets and mongooses. Jackals are also present. These mammals play crucial roles in the ecosystem, from seed dispersal to population control of other species.
# Urban Wildlife in Islamabad

Islamabad’s green belts and urban forests are home to a range of fauna adapting to human proximity.

## Notable Urban Species

- **Red Fox (Vulpes vulpes)**: Adapts well to urban fringes.
- **Indian Grey Mongoose**: Frequently spotted near trash dumps and water channels.
- **Jackals**: Their howls can be heard in sectors bordering MHNP.
- **Kites, Crows, and Mynas**: Thrive in the urban core due to food availability.

## Human-Wildlife Conflict

Incidents involving leopards near Trail 6 and the accidental killing of snakes have raised public concern. IWMB promotes coexistence strategies like:

- Wildlife-safe garbage disposal
- Not feeding monkeys or birds
- Reporting animal sightings instead of confronting them

Awareness drives and community sessions are key elements of urban biodiversity management.
