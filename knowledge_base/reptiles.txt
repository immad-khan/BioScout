The reptile population in the Margalla Hills includes various snake and lizard species. Common snakes found here include the Indian Cobra, Russell's Viper, and various non-venomous species like the Rat Snake. Lizards such as geckos and skinks are also frequently seen. Reptiles are an important part of the food web, controlling insect and rodent populations. It's always best to observe them from a distance and avoid handling them, especially snakes.
