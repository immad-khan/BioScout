The Margalla Hills National Park is a significant habitat for various bird species, making it a popular spot for birdwatching. Common residents include the House Crow, Common Myna, Rock Pigeon, and various species of babblers. During migration seasons, many migratory birds also pass through or temporarily reside in the area, contributing to its avian diversity. Conservation efforts focus on protecting their nesting sites and food sources. Some raptors like kites and eagles can also be spotted soaring above the hills.
